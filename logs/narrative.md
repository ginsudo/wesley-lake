## 2026-09-15 *(written up by Claude)*
Has been watching the birds at Wesley Lake since moving to Asbury Park this past
winter, especially from March onward. It's been a joy watching the two adult mute
swans that were there mate and produce four cygnets, which have been observed
growing to nearly adult size over the months. Has enjoyed learning to distinguish
ducks, geese, cormorants, and swans from each other. Knows blue herons are present
at the lake but isn't sure whether they've personally spotted one yet.
